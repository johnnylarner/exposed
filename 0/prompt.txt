i'm reviewing the parquet component. There are a few issues here:
- the begin/finish mechanism is reliant on the caller for it to work. Much like with a DB TX, we don't want to expose that to the service.
- second, i like how you used the decoder to create batch records. But finish_declaration_capture is hard to reason about. We don't need this level of complexity with manifests and anyhow errors.
- generally we are overengineering this component. Simple is best. We don't need to hack solutions for very unlikely edge cases
