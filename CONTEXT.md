# Declarations

The project helps people understand declarations made by Members of Parliament
and the funding recorded in them.

## Language

**Declaration**:
A statement of an MP's interests that can contain several funding entries.
The same declaration can be associated with more than one MP.

**MP association**:
The relationship between a declaration and an MP who made it.

**Funding entry**:
One instance of financial compensation or support recorded in a declaration,
using the latest published details.
_Avoid_: Source field group

**Latest version**:
The declaration's most recent register publication, which supplies its current
funding entries.
